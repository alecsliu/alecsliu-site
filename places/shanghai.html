Shanghai

Shanghai is objectively the most beautiful city in the world. This is a matter of pure fact, and is uninfluenced by my family's generations long tenure in Shanghai or my love of Shanghainese culture and language. If I were a penguin born and raised in Cape Town, I think I would insist that Shanghai is the most wonderful place on Earth.

Hotels

  • Capella ShanghaiXuhui

    Whether visiting or living in Shanghai, we always stayed in our own family's apartment or our relatives'. The Capella was the first time I've ever stayed in a hotel in Shanghai, and as far as hotels go, look no further. Everything about this place is fantastic (including them helping us source some snacks my dad loved growing up). The hotel is a love letter to Shanghai: little cartoon books my parents grew up reading, a historic 石库门 (called za ku men in Shanghainese), and a Shanghainese breakfast that I remember eating during summers in Shanghai. There are other fantastic hotels in Shanghai, but I can't imagine anywhere else with such a sense of Shanghai.

Museums & sites

  • Shanghai Jewish Refugees MuseumHongkou

    A wonderful museum located near where my family used to live, with an incredibly interesting history of the Jewish refugees who found safety in Shanghai. Shocking line from the museum: “Though they lived in a room without water supply, Ellen's mother still hired a domestic worker and a Chinese Amah to take care of her.”

Shopping

  • SongmontHuangpu

    I love Songmont's aesthetics and quality of service. There's a decent discount compared to buying outside of China, though nothing terribly significant. Definitely worth a visit, and Huaihai Zhong Lu is a major shopping area anyway.

  • Xiling Seal Art SocietyChenghuangmiao

    Located in the Chenghuangmiao area, it's an outpost of the famous Hangzhou society and will do seal carving and sell related supplies. A good place to get a souvenir, and you'll probably visit anyway, because Chenghuangmiao.

  • SwatchThe Bund

    Located on Nanjing Road (another place that will almost certainly be visited), this is a great place to see some interesting Swatches and pick up something fun as a souvenir. I ended up getting one of the Year of the Horse Swatches while here.

Restaurants

  • Ren He Guan (人和馆)Xuhui

    Reasonably priced and one of the few Michelin star restaurants in Shanghai worth checking out. (Don't rely on the Michelin Guide in China: most of the options in Shanghai aren't even Shanghainese, but from Canton.) Service was fast and the food was excellent.

  • Fu 1088Jing'an

    Private rooms in a beautiful villa, with all of the “refined” ingredients that make up a Chinese upper class palate. Growing up far from that, I have no interest in ingesting more sea cucumber or fish maw in my life, but it's a good representation of all of that, and the service and technique are all on point. The crab season here is supposedly fantastic, though we did not opt into that (maybe next time!).
