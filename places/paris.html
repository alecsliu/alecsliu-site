Paris

Paris is every American's idea of the place to be, and I am not immune to American ideas of elegance. Growing up, I always wanted to visit Paris, so while studying abroad in London, I ended up visiting 8 times, plus a few times since college. Paris is definitely not perfect, but I love it dearly.

Paris is big enough that the well-known sights are worth doing, unless you already know one isn't for you: Sacré-Cœur, Montparnasse, the Eiffel Tower, the Panthéon, Les Invalides, the Centre Pompidou.

Bookstores

  • L'Écume des PagesSaint-Germain-des-Prés

    This bookstore in Saint-Germain-des-Prés is very cute and has lovely staff. They helped me source a French copy of Latronico's Perfection and were enjoyable to chat with. It does have an English section, but it's not terribly huge, so I'd skip it if you don't have an interest in French books.

Museums & sites

  • Palais de Tokyo16e

    The museum was closed when I went, but the bookstore is worth a stop on its own. I picked up The Tehraner there, a small book of New Yorker style covers by Iranian artists.

  • Fondation Louis VuittonBois de Boulogne

    A Gehry building with terraces that look out to La Défense. When I went, they were exhibiting the Fayet collection, with strong post-impressionist work; the standout was a Redon taken from an abbey. It's on the edge of the city.

Restaurants

Heads up: mostly meat.

  • David Toutain7e

    Two Michelin stars, with lunch menus that are reasonably priced for that level. In 2019 it was the cheapest restaurant in Paris with two stars.

  • Jacques Faussat17e

    This is a small and intimate French restaurant with a Michelin star (who doesn't have one in Paris though, to be honest). Overall, everything was flawless (technically speaking), though portion sizes were small for the tasting. Definitely not on the touristy side, and a nice, quiet place to have a meal.

  • Le Petit Pontoise5e

    The rabbit here was excellent when I went in 2021. Cannot guarantee anything else, but the location is convenient and I have fond memories of this place.
