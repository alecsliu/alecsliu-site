Madrid

Madrid will always have a special place in my heart as the first place I ever went to in Europe. Granted, at the time, we stayed with my cousins in what was only legally in the Community of Madrid, much like Lancaster is in Los Angeles (County), but there's something special about Madrid: a sense of place I rarely feel elsewhere.

Hotels

  • Palacio de TepaBarrio de las Letras

    I used to stay here before the recent price increases. The location is great, the service is fairly decent, and upgrades are fairly common. Don't stay here during high season, but during calmer months, it's a fantastic option.

Shopping

  • IvorypressChamberí

    I love this bookstore: a unique set of books, both their own and other art books in general. When I visited, I spent an hour browsing, and my only regret is the 3 or 4 books I had my eye on but didn't buy. They were out of stock of Madrid. Libro de Libros from their Cities collection, but they had New York Memories (which I purchased).

  • LoeweRecoletos

    Big discounts are available (at least as of 2024) if you buy direct, between getting the VAT back as a tourist and other savings. The store is constantly busy (as one might expect), but it's worth visiting if you like Loewe.

Restaurants & cafés

  • GaytánChamartín

    Solo trips sometimes include the awkwardness of trying to find a reservation at a nice restaurant that doesn't refuse to admit someone who has the audacity to eat alone. Gaytán features a creative menu and hits that great medium between “always busy” and “not so busy you have to reserve a month in advance” (and of course fairly standard prices for a tasting).

  • Chocolatería San GinésSol

    Yes, this shit is touristy as hell. Please don't go if you see a long line. But having taken many, many awkward flights into Madrid, knowing San Ginés will be open and a convenient stop before I get a proper breakfast has been a wonderful experience on more than one occasion.

Misc

  • Casa de VelázquezCiudad Universitaria

    Host to the French in Madrid. The house is free to visit, with an exhibition on the first floor (sometimes). Be prepared for a bonjour, but the property is beautiful and a nice stop if you're in the area. I would not recommend going out of the way to visit.

  • KapitalAtocha

    Expensive compared to the rest of Madrid, but having experienced NYC nightlife, I'll pay again for Kapital.

  • Green Tiger TattooCentro

    Got a nice tattoo done here at incredibly reasonable prices. Conveniently located as well.
