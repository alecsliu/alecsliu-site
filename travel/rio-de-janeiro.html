Rio de Janeiro

I lived in Leblon for an extended stay in winter 2026 (their summer).

Restaurants

  • SanLeblon

    An incredibly popular restaurant in the heart of Leblon. The lunch menu (menu executivo) is extremely reasonable for the quality and quantity of sushi, and it was one of my favorite places to take visitors.

  • Ferro e FarinhaLeblon

    This Brazilian pizza chain has locations across the city, with the Ipanema one being the snazziest. I prefer the location on top of Shopping Leblon because it tends to have the shortest lines and was closest to me. It's also particularly great if you have vegetarian or vegan friends, because the coconut curry pizza is legitimately one of the best innovative takes on pizza I've ever had (and almost all their pizzas are fantastic). The drinks here are great too.

  • Giuseppe Grill

    My favorite place to get a nice steak in Rio. Service can be a bit slow, so I wouldn't plan around it, but the atmosphere is good and the food is excellent. They also sell custom plates for their specialty dishes as souvenirs (I wish I'd bought one).

Hikes & activities

  • Morro Dois IrmãosVidigal

    While this hike does involve going through a favela, the entire process is very streamlined and simple. Moto-taxis will pick you up at a standard price, and once you're at the start of the park, navigating to the summit is very straightforward. The sunrise hike is worth it (no need for an Airbnb guide), but make sure to bring bug repellent and flashlights. The view from Dois Irmãos is, I believe, one of the most beautiful things you can see on Earth.

Shopping

  • Casa FidalgusIpanema

    This small shop in Ipanema has a lot of unique, locally produced specialty goods (mostly consumables, like spirits). Some great, unique cachaça is sold here that I wouldn't recommend wasting in a caipirinha.
