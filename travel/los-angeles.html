Alec Liu/Travel/Los Angeles

Los Angeles

As someone currently living on the Westside, most of my suggestions will be for places near where I live. This is a non-exhaustive list of places I like.

Bookstores

  • ArcanaCulver City

    An extensive collection of great art books one can get lost in for hours. Dedicated parking and metered parking are both available, and usually open outside of the weekends.

  • Village WellCulver City

    A bookstore with a large café and co-working space. The book selection is fairly classic, and features a surprisingly extensive collection of Spanish-language books for Culver City.

Restaurants & cafés

  • SimpangVenice & Palms

    Both locations (I usually go to Venice) feature slightly different menus, but are tasty takes on Indonesian cuisine and well worth the money. I always try to get one of the tastings when visiting.

  • American BeautySanta Monica

    Another multi-location restaurant, though I've only ever been to the Santa Monica one. Noah used to live walking distance from here, so we'd go every month. The steak options are very reasonably priced, and the quality and cook are consistently excellent. The apple pie (during the season) has a wonderfully flaky crust, and the restaurant itself is outdoors and has a cozy vibe.

  • Guelaguetza

    Authentic Oaxacan isn't super common, even in Los Angeles, but Guelaguetza has extensive Oaxacan offerings, and their tlayudas are always solid picks.

  • Bistro Na'sTemple City · San Gabriel Valley

    All the best Chinese food these days is far out in the San Gabriel Valley, so I make the drive whenever I can. Bistro Na's is where good Chinese sons and daughters bring their parents for a traditional, fine dining experience. Exotic Chinese ingredients aren't really my thing (fish maw, sea cucumber, etc.), but if you want to try faithful preparations, this is the place to do so. Otherwise, we almost always order the crispy shrimp and the diced black pepper honey Angus beef.

Misc

  • Flax Pen to PaperWestwood

    A shop filled with stationery supplies and pens. The staff are very helpful and happy to guide you, whether it's through your first fountain pen purchase or when buying a “grail” pen.
